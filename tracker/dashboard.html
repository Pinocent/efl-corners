How this works, and what the terms mean
Prediction
The model's estimate for a match: expected corners for each side, and the chance of each market. Every match gets one.
Call
Landed / missed
Whether a call came off once the result was in. Calls freeze at kick-off and are never changed afterwards.
Chance
How likely the model thinks something is. 60% means it should happen about 6 times in 10.
Fair odds
1 ÷ chance. A bookmaker price above fair may be value. Below fair, you're paying their margin.
Corner handicap
A head start in corners. "Stockport −1.5" means Stockport need to win the corner count by 2 or more. "Peterborough +1.5" means Peterborough can lose it by 1 and still win the bet.
Line
xG
Expected goals. The model blends each side's shot quality, goals and the bookmakers' prices.
Clean sheet
The side concedes no goals.
BTTS
Both teams score.
Gameweek
Matches played within a few days of each other, with no club playing twice. Worked out from the dates, so Thursday–Monday and Tuesday–Wednesday rounds both group correctly.

Where the model is strongest. Across the last two seasons it was right about which side wins more corners in about 76% of confident cases. Corner totals are much harder to call; it only slightly beats using the league average. That's why most calls are handicaps.

How corners are predicted. Team ratings for corners won and conceded, and for shots, each with a home and away adjustment. Recent matches count more. Bookmakers' match odds adjust how corners split between the sides.

How goals are predicted. xG and goals blended with the goals implied by the bookmakers' prices, on a score grid that corrects for the extra 0–0 and 1–1 draws a simpler model misses.

League 2 markets only offer Over lines, so League 2 shows no Under or handicap calls.
