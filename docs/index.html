Reading this board

Gameweeks are worked out from the fixture dates. Matches on days close together form one round, and no club plays twice in a round. Thu–Mon and Tue–Wed rounds, Friday starts and Boxing Day all group correctly without a fixed rule.

Expected corners come from team attack and defence ratings for corners and shots, with a home and away adjustment for each club. Recent matches count more, and last season's ratings give a starting point. The bookmakers' 1X2 odds adjust how the corners split between the two sides.

Totals are hard to call. Across the last two seasons, expected totals only just beat quoting the league average. The model is much better at who wins more corners: strong calls landed about 76% of the time. Handicaps and big gaps are its strongest markets.

Goals blend each side's xG and goal record with the goals implied by the bookmakers' prices. BTTS and clean sheets use a Dixon–Coles score grid, which corrects the usual under-count of 0–0 and 1–1.

Calls are predictions above a set confidence level: . League 2 only shows Over lines, and handicaps appear in the Championship and League 1.

Fair odds are 1 ÷ probability. A price above fair may be value. A price below fair means you are paying the bookmaker's margin.
